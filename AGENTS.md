# Project guidance

This is a static React/Vite frontend with a PHP JSON API and MySQL database for InfinityFree. Read README.md for development, import, monthly refresh, and deployment instructions.

## Data handling

- Keep the distinction between coordinate markers and official parcel boundaries explicit. The current coordinate workbook has points only.
- The optional Boundaries mode reads a separate immutable geometry snapshot built from the 24 official KAIS SHP exports, linked only by exact cadastral ID. Preserve full Polygon/MultiPolygon rings and holes, with no repair, buffering, inferred shapes or display simplification. WGS84 coordinates are transformed for display; published native AREA/PERIM remain source values. A map layer is not a certified sketch or field survey. Quarantine conflicting or invalid IDs explicitly and retain point fallback. Boundary viewport results remain all-or-nothing under their size/count limits; low zoom and dense views use complete point/cluster fallback. The mode preference stays in the browser and all existing search/document/cadastral filters still apply.
- Preserve workbook names internally for import audit. Public plot provenance shows the official register name/URL, snapshot date when known, and exact-ID matching method, without Excel filenames (user preference).
- Do not join documents by address or geographic proximity.
- Cadastral area and basic attributes come from the 24 official Sofia land-parcel ZIP exports in KAIS Open Data and join only by exact ID. Preserve published AREA as geometry area in m²; never substitute a building area or claim certified sketch area. Do not import owner names. Preserve conflicting IDs with all safe variants and exclude them from area filters.
- Cadastral district, purpose, permanent use and property category are separate from document district and description keywords. Facet selections are OR within one field and AND across fields, area and document conditions. Preserve exact official labels/codes with stable keys; exclude unknown values only in an actively constrained field. Cadastral conditions remain usable with no linked construction record.
- Do not describe workbook snapshots as live or complete government data.
- Business registered-office associations are separate from construction documents and parcel ownership. Import all numbered company export parts from one pinned source revision, require a confirmed checksum-valid EIK and a Sofia legal-entity registered address, and exclude sole traders/natural-person records. Publish only allowlisted business fields; raw address remainders, contacts, personal identifiers and Other_Data role text stay private. Exact cadastral-address matches require the complete normalized street and exact simple number identifying one parcel, including unknown street-kind union checks. Municipal address/quarter/block records and contextual historical street names require exact official reference evidence. Attach a parcel only when every candidate address point is strictly inside the same valid non-transport parcel, in native and published geometry; ambiguous/boundary/outside/conflicting cases never snap to a nearby parcel. A single unambiguous official address point can appear separately without a parcel. Preserve match methods, source dates, CRS basis, attribution and separate coverage counts. A registered office establishes neither property ownership nor actual activity there.
- The Companies map uses parcel interior representative points and a separate official municipal address index. Label parcel associations and address-only locations distinctly; never open construction/parcel evidence for an address-only ID. Preserve holes/disconnected parts, no geometry repair or fabricated coordinates. Clusters retain complete location/company totals; all/parcel/address levels and name/EIK search apply to the entire map, independently of search previews. Construction filters remain preserved under Properties. Keep three main workspace tabs available and nested scrolling confined to its sidebar.
- Quarterly registered-sales statistics come only from the Registry Agency's official PDF reports, exact Sofia office row. Counts cover all real-estate types in that office's jurisdiction, not unique homes, apartments, municipal/neighborhood totals or prices. Preserve per-quarter source/hash/publication/retrieval evidence and revisions; display archive gaps as unknown. Year-on-year comparisons need the exact prior-year quarter and trailing-four-quarter totals require contiguous observations. Mortgage registration counts do not measure the share of financed purchases. Keep this series distinct from NSI housing-price indices and asking prices; do not claim predictive accuracy without evaluation on later unseen periods.
- Submitted search phrases must constrain every matching viewport point and cluster, independently of the forty-result preview. Match original scalar field values and identifiers case-insensitively, exclude workbook/source audit names from text search, and combine phrases at parcel level with all/any modes. Keep search values out of analytics. Preserve submitted phrases when opening results or selecting no-linked-records.
- Do not add credentials to frontend code, generated SQL, source control, or public files. Use public/private/config.php only on the host; it is excluded from git.

## Hosting

- Target InfinityFree's static-file, PHP, and MySQL environment. Do not add Base44 auth, entities, or deploy dependencies.
- The user authorized monthly GitHub Actions parsing and verified FTPS deployment of a prepared private register dataset. GitHub performs the parsing; hosted PHP reads the completed file. Do not simulate cron by calling public write URLs or expose a SQL importer.
- Keep the public PHP API read-only. MySQL retains coordinate data and the original imported observations. New register evidence lives in immutable private/registry-snapshots/ directories selected by private/registry-active.json; private/registry-data.json is the original fallback. Legacy workbook coordinates remain a fallback only. When the complete boundary v2 parcel catalogue is active, its source-observed ID set and strictly verified interior points drive current map/search/meta independently of MySQL; no coordinate SQL import is required for new official parcels.
- A refresh must match each register's stated row total and finish all three core sources before publication. Merge observations by source/document hash without deleting earlier evidence. Preserve legacy certificate evidence separately, with an unknown snapshot date where none is recorded.
- The monthly/manual workflow also captures and validates all 24 cadastral exports before publication. Cadastral files are immutable under private/cadastre-snapshots/, selected by private/cadastre-active.json. Register and cadastral activation are individually atomic; do not describe them as one transaction. The active boundary v2 catalogue updates the current parcel ID universe and map points automatically. Workbook coordinate imports remain legacy fallback data.
- An independent reusable official-cadastre job in the monthly workflow captures all 24 official exports, publishes attributes and builds boundaries plus the complete ID catalogue from those same verified archives and publishes immutable files under private/boundary-snapshots/ selected by private/boundaries-active.json. Boundary activation is a third independent atomic publication. The standalone Refresh official Sofia parcel catalogue and boundaries workflow can also be run manually; it publishes attributes first, then atomically activates the geometry and catalogue together through the boundary pointer. A register parser failure does not block this independent job. Preserve previous pointers and immutable snapshots; never publish owner data. The bbox lookup index uses conservative outward 6-decimal bounds under the 10,000,000-byte hosting limit; display geometry keeps all vertices at 7 decimal places. Current limits: 600 combined viewport features/fallback points, 100,000 vertices, 3 MiB response, 1 MiB geometry shards and 256 MiB prepared snapshot.
- FTPS must verify certificates and encrypt data transfers. Verify each uploaded immutable file's SHA-256, preserve the previous private pointer or fallback, and replace the active pointer by rename. Stop on failed verification, unexpected layout, an incomplete capture, or the documented manifest/index/shard/processing limits. Never delete or overwrite an existing immutable snapshot directory.
- Official sales aggregates are a bounded public data/transactions-context.json file, refreshed by an independent monthly job or the manual transaction-statistics workflow. Fetch and validate hosted history before merging; failed captures preserve the existing file. Dedicated publishers share a concurrency group. Ordinary frontend publication only bootstraps an absent transaction snapshot and always preserves an existing validated hosted one. This does not change the separate NSI context publication rules.
- Company address refresh is an independent monthly/manual job. Publish only validated immutable private/business-snapshots/ files and activate private/business-active.json after all byte/hash checks; retain the previous pointer. public/business-data.php is read-only and bounded. Never put company CSVs into the website, browser bundle or repository. Growth adds numbered export parts automatically from the pinned commit.

## Asking-price market and address lookup

- The daily imot.bg collector captures public sale listings for two-, three- and four-room apartments in the source's Sofia catalogue, including nearby settlements and unknown prices. Use one paced worker and require every uncapped search leaf's stable declared total to match unique regular listing IDs. Stop on access challenges or incomplete captures. Do not claim all Sofia properties or all categories are covered.
- Publish minimal factual fields and original advert links, excluding descriptions, photos and private-seller contacts. The user explicitly authorized public professional agency names, profile links and advertised business phone/site details. Publish them only from a confirmed professional seller DOM block, with checked date and closed-schema URL/phone validation; never extract private contact details or broker personal names. Listing IDs are not unique property IDs. Advertised construction/Act 16 claims are not official cadastral evidence. Never join adverts or geocoder locations to official parcels by address or proximity.
- Compare asking EUR/m² only within neighborhood, apartment type, VAT basis and construction-age cohort. Exclude the candidate from its peers. Potential bargains require >=20% below the peer median and >=8 other comparable ads; differences >=60% require review and do not alert. Unknown currency is never inferred; unknown VAT has a separate labeled comparison and cannot generate default alerts. An explicit local preference may include the unreported-VAT comparison group, with its unknown final tax amount stated. Such alerts still require eligible comparisons and unchanged area/price basis.
- Historical captures and the first completed daily baseline cannot generate daily alerts. Suppress alerts for captures older than 48 hours. Preferences and seen event keys stay in the user's browser; alerts appear only on this website while it is opened. No messaging credentials are needed.
- Market-only retention is an exception to the no-deletion rule above: after verified activation and verification of current/previous pointers, retain the latest seven completed market snapshots plus any referenced current/previous snapshot. Delete only fully verified, older, unreferenced allowlisted UUID directories under private/market-snapshots/. Leave unverifiable incomplete trees intact; stop on unexpected layout. Never prune registry or cadastral evidence. Private Actions capture/snapshot artifacts are retained 31 days; private price history is bounded to 90 days/64 changes per listing and aggregate history to 180 completed captures.
- geocode.php accepts manual address submissions and uses a fixed, trusted HTTPS provider. No autocomplete or parser/bulk use. Its private hashed-query response cache and global rate lock are incidental provider-policy state permitted under the read-only API rule; no dataset writes or cadastral joins. Preserve provider attribution, consent-free analytics exclusion of addresses, TLS verification, global pacing and bounded cache. A compatible provider can be changed only by trusted host configuration.
- The primary search field routes recognizable addresses to map navigation, independently of record-term limits and invalid record filters. Address submissions are not parcel-filter phrases. Offer explicit address/record overrides for ambiguous text, preserve other filters, and prevent obsolete asynchronous searches from changing the map after a newer user action. Consume queued geocoder requests once across mobile Sheet remounts. Auto-select only one distinct returned location; retain a chooser for ambiguous locations.
- Listing map locations are approximate grouped street/quarter references. Never use agency office addresses or nearby amenity mentions as property addresses. The offline safe reference discards exact parcel IDs and house numbers; ads never join to an official parcel. Sparse or ambiguous references remain unknown, with dated source attribution and uncertainty circles. Daily ad refreshes do not change the reference's capture date.
- Seller labels require evidence: a professional profile for agency, an explicit private seller claim for private, unknown for missing/conflicting evidence. No private-ownership verification is implied. Keep descriptions/contacts transient; retain only allowlisted derived claims, hashes and checked dates. Full-description coverage is incremental and must remain visible; its optional failure does not invalidate a completed price census.
- Method 2 comparisons additionally separate completion stage, area basis and floor band, constrain area to ±25%, match condition when known, and prefer a same-reference-street sample only with >=8 peers. Estimates use asking median × advertised area with peer quartiles, never a transaction valuation or confidence interval. Exceptional sale terms and conflicting completion claims suppress bargain signals. Comparison-attribute changes suppress daily alerts; method changes rebaseline alerts while preserving price history. All producer/publisher/PHP shard bounds must agree (currently512).

- Listing comparison method 3 additionally separates construction, furnishing and elevator evidence, uses actual built/commissioned year within ±10 and numeric floor within ±2 when known, and reports missing factors, peer sample and P25–P75 price position. Planned years never imply building age. These are comparable asking-price guides; unknown sale prices preclude a claim of verified fair value. Method changes rebaseline alerts.
- Buyer budgets distinguish price/VAT and all buyer costs. Default assumed agency commission is 3% including VAT, private seller 0%, unknown seller explicitly assumed agency; all are editable. Sofia acquisition tax is a selected 3% municipality model, not inferred for all catalogue settlements. Use the higher of full consideration and entered tax valuation, progressive notary tariff with separately shown VAT, 0.1% registration/minimum €5.11, explicit fee sharing and optional drafting/extra costs. Unknown property VAT requires two scenarios. Keep official sources/check date visible and per-property prices/valuations/extras out of shared storage.

- Cadastral catalogue membership follows the complete 24 Sofia (68134) land-parcel exports, including IDs with quarantined/unusable geometry. New source IDs appear without MySQL rows; absent IDs leave the ordinary current map; retained document/legacy coordinate evidence stays available through exact historical lookup. Immutable older catalogues remain for audit without implying a complete public lineage register. Label absence only as not present in the latest downloaded snapshot, never proof of legal cancellation. Parcel splits/mergers do not transfer parent documents or infer successor relationships. Building/apartment suffix identifiers and other settlement codes are outside this land-parcel catalogue. Show the source capture date and interior-point provenance; do not call this a live official lookup. The bounded catalogue TSV and full polygons share one immutable snapshot/pointer, with previous source membership verified before reporting added/absent counts.

- Keep the asking-price overview under its existing 2 MiB limit. It contains complete neighborhood/VAT statistics and exact cohort counts, not thousands of unused fine-grained cohort arrays. Full offer comparisons remain in the paginated listing shards. Preview counts must reconcile with full candidate totals and cannot bound the actual alert feed.
- A successful website asset/reader check does not establish price freshness. Verify the active private market pointer, summary hash/counts and original captured_at after publication. Require a daily capture within 48 hours when certifying fresh prices; report stale/historical state explicitly otherwise. Preserve TLS certificate validation, including provider expiry failures.
- Enriched capture recovery is manual and restricted to an original same-repository main-branch daily run whose capture and enrichment steps both succeeded. Require artifact SHA-256, strict bounded ZIP members, unchanged source identities/prices/context and current closed enrichment schemas, with the original source capture no more than six hours old. Preserve source timestamps and the original bytes; never relabel recovered evidence as a new crawl.
